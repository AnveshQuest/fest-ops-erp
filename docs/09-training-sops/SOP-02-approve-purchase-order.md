# SOP-02 · Review and approve a purchase order

**Who:** Finance Head (all POs), Faculty-in-charge (POs above ₹1,00,000) · **When:** daily, or from the email alert

1. Open the PO from the email link, or search **Purchase Order** → filter *Workflow State = Pending Finance Review*. `[screenshot]`
2. Check: right supplier, right department, rates match the quote, quotes attached if above ₹50,000.
3. Check the department's budget: **Milan Department Spend Tracker** (see SOP-06).
4. Click **Actions**:
   - **Approve** (up to ₹1,00,000) → PO is submitted and the budget is committed.
   - **Escalate** (above ₹1,00,000) → goes to the faculty-in-charge.
   - **Reject** → add a comment saying what to fix.
5. Faculty: open the email, click **Approve** or **Reject** (log in if asked).

### If something goes wrong
- *"Budget exceeded" error on approve* → the PO would take the department over budget. Reject it, or ask the Coordinators for a **Budget Revision** first.
- *No Approve button* → you created this PO yourself; a second Finance Head user must approve it.
