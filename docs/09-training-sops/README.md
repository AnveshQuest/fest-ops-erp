# Training SOPs

One page per task, written for the team that would run Milan on this system. Screenshots (`[screenshot]` markers) are taken from the rebuild's live site; the demo video follows the same steps.

| SOP | Task | Who | Training time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SOP-01](SOP-01-raise-purchase-request.md) | Raise a purchase request and draft a PO | Dept Heads | 15 min |
| [SOP-02](SOP-02-approve-purchase-order.md) | Review and approve a PO | Finance Head, Faculty | 10 min |
| [SOP-03](SOP-03-artist-contract-and-advance.md) | Record an artist contract and pay the advance | Pronites Head, Finance Head | 15 min |
| [SOP-04](SOP-04-sponsor-invoice-and-payment.md) | Invoice a sponsor and record payment | Sponsorship Head, Finance Head | 15 min |
| [SOP-05](SOP-05-merch-pos.md) | Sell merchandise at the counter and close the day | Merch volunteers | 10 min |
| [SOP-06](SOP-06-check-department-budget.md) | Check a department's budget | Everyone | 5 min |

**Proposed training plan for a future team:** one 30-minute session per role group, hands-on with sample data; everyone completes one task before leaving. In the rebuild, each SOP is walked through end to end as its test user before the demo is recorded.
