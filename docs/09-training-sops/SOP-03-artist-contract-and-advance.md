# SOP-03 · Record an artist contract and pay the advance

**Who:** Pronites Head (contract), Finance Head (payment)

### A. Purchase order (Pronites Head)
1. Create a PO for the agency/artist with item **Artist Performance Fee**, cost center *Pronites*.
2. Payment Terms Template: **Artist 50-50**. Set the second term's due date to the **performance date**.
3. Submit for review (SOP-01). Artist POs above ₹1,00,000 go to the faculty-in-charge.

### B. Contract (Pronites Head)
1. Search **Contract** → **+ Add**. Party Type *Supplier*, Party = the agency.
2. Contract Template: *Artist Performance Agreement*. Fill **Performance Date**, **Performance Slot**, **Rider Summary**.
3. Under **References**, link the approved PO. Attach the signed contract PDF.
4. When signed, tick **Signed**, add Signee and Signed On. Status becomes *Active*. `[screenshot]`

### C. Advance payment (Finance Head)
1. Open the approved PO → **Create → Payment**.
2. Amount = 50% advance; enter the UTR/cheque number and date. Submit.

### On the show day
Book the artist's invoice from the PO (**Create → Purchase Invoice**) and pay the balance against it.
