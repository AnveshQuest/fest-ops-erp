# SOP-01 · Raise a purchase request and draft a PO

**Who:** Department Heads · **When:** any time your department needs to spend money

### A. Raise the request
1. Log in at the Milan ERP link (works on phone).
2. Search **Material Request** → **+ Add**. Purpose: *Purchase*.
3. Add items: pick the item (e.g. *Bus Hire (per day)*), quantity, and **Required By** date. Your department is filled in automatically. `[screenshot]`
4. If you see a **budget warning**, talk to the Finance Head before going further.
5. **Save** and **Submit**.

### B. Draft the purchase order (once the vendor is chosen)
1. Open your Material Request → **Create → Purchase Order**.
2. Choose the supplier. Check rates and quantities.
3. Above **₹50,000**: collect three quotations, tick **3 Quotes Compared** and attach the comparison (a photo or PDF of the three quotes is fine). `[screenshot]`
4. **Save**, then click **Actions → Submit for Review**.

### Done when
The PO shows *Pending Finance Review*. You'll get an email when it's approved or rejected.

### If something goes wrong
- *"Submit for Review" missing* → amount is above ₹50,000 and quotes aren't ticked/attached.
- *Rejected* → read the comment, fix it, click **Actions → Revise**, then submit again.
