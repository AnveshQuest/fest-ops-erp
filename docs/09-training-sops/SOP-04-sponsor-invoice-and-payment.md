# SOP-04 · Invoice a sponsor and record payment

**Who:** Sponsorship Head (invoice), Finance Head (payments)

### A. Sponsor record (once per sponsor)
1. Search **Customer** → **+ Add**. Customer Group *Sponsors*, **Sponsorship Tier**, Default Payment Terms *Sponsor 50-50*.
2. Add the sponsor's contact person and billing address.

### B. Invoice
1. Search **Sales Invoice** → **+ Add**. Customer = sponsor.
2. Item: *Title / Associate / Event Sponsorship*, rate = agreed amount.
3. Check the **Payment Schedule** (two instalments). Attach the signed MoU.
4. Submit and **Email** the invoice to the sponsor from the form. `[screenshot]`

### C. When money arrives (Finance Head)
1. Open the invoice → **Create → Payment**. Enter amount received, reference number and date. Submit.

### Weekly follow-up
Open **Accounts Receivable** → filter Customer Group *Sponsors*. Anyone in the 30+ days column gets a call that week.
