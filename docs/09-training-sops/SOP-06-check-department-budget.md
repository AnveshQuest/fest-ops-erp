# SOP-06 · Check a department's budget

**Takes:** under 2 minutes

### Finance Head, Coordinators, Faculty
1. Search **Milan Department Spend Tracker**.
2. Filters: Company *Milan 2025*, dates = the fest's financial year. `[screenshot]`
3. Each row shows:
   - **Budget** — what the department was given
   - **Committed** — approved POs not yet billed
   - **Actual** — bills and reimbursements already booked
   - **Available** — what can still be spent
4. Any department above **80% used** gets a call before its next request is approved.

### Department Heads
1. Search **Budget Variance Report**. Your cost center is pre-selected. `[screenshot]`
2. It shows budget vs actual for your department. Ask the Finance Head for the committed figure, or check your approved POs (Purchase Order list → filter *Status = To Bill*).

(The Spend Tracker reads the database directly, so it isn't limited by department; that's why only Finance, Coordinators and Faculty get it.)
