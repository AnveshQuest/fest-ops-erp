# 01 · Stakeholder Review

A rebuild is only credible if people who ran Milan 2025 recognise the problems it describes and find the solution workable. This step gets that check from 1–2 of them.

## Who to ask

1. **Milan 2025 Finance Head / Treasurer** — saw every payment and the budget sheet.
2. **One Overall Coordinator** — saw approvals and overruns across departments.
3. Optional: another department head (Production or Hospitality spend the most after Pronites).

## Message to send

> Hi [Name], I'm rebuilding Milan 2025's budget, purchasing and sponsor tracking in ERPNext (an open-source ERP) as a portfolio project — how it *should* have worked, based on what we went through.
>
> Could you give me 20 minutes twice? First to check my map of how money actually moved in 2025, and later to look at a 10-minute demo and tell me whether it would have helped. I'll anonymise every name and amount, and I won't use anything you share without asking.

## Session 1 · Check the AS-IS (20 min)

- Walk through the five AS-IS maps in [`04-process-maps.md`](04-process-maps.md). For each: "Is this how it worked? What's missing?"
- Confirm or correct the reconstructed baseline numbers in [`11-metrics.md`](11-metrics.md).
- Ask the top three things they'd fix if they ran Milan again.

## Session 2 · Demo review (20 min)

Show the demo flow from [`data/sample/demo-transactions.md`](../data/sample/demo-transactions.md):

1. Pronites budget → a sound vendor PO goes to Finance for approval.
2. An artist booking that would break the artist budget gets blocked.
3. Artist contract with 50/50 payment terms and an advance.
4. Sponsor invoice → part payment → receivables ageing.
5. Department Spend Tracker for every department.

Then ask:
- Would this have changed anything in Milan 2025? What exactly?
- What would the department heads have pushed back on?
- What's missing that the fest really needed?
