Fest Ops ERP

I ran pronites for Milan 2025, held in October at IIT Hyderabad as its inter-hostel General Championship, and watched its money move through WhatsApp and spreadsheets. This is a rebuild of how it should have run in ERPNext: ten departments, one budget each, every purchase approved and every sponsor rupee tracked.

A retrospective rebuild, not a live deployment. All figures are anonymised and reconstructed.

How Milan 2025's money moved

  • Requests and approvals lived in WhatsApp threads and email.
  • The budget sheet fell behind the moment a vendor was paid.
  • Artist contracts and advances sat in inboxes, including mine.
  • Sponsor payments were chased from memory after the fest.
  • Merch was sold for cash and UPI with a manual tally.

The question I couldn't answer quickly as Head of Pronites: how much can we still spend, counting what we've already promised vendors?

One path for every rupee spent

In the rebuild, department heads raise requests from their phones. Above ₹50,000 the order can't move without three quotations attached. Above ₹1,00,000 it goes to the faculty-in-charge, who approves from the email. Nobody can approve their own order, and the budget check runs at approval.

26 requirements, 1 custom report

Spending approvalsMaterial request, supplier quotations, purchase order with a 3-level workflow
Artist bookingsContract linked to the order, 50% advance and 50% on the show day
Sponsor moneySales invoices with two instalments, receivables ageing
MerchandiseStock by size, POS with cash and UPI, day-end closing
